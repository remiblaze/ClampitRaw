# Clampit Raw — one-knob peak limiter

![Clampit Raw](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/RemiBlaze/ClampitRaw/main/clampitraw-ui-screenshot.png)

**Pin your peaks and add loudness with a single knob.**

Clampit Raw is the free, one-knob version of **Clampit** — turn one dial and your track gets tighter, denser, and louder-feeling, while staying level-matched so it never jumps in volume.

Fully **signed and notarized** for macOS as **AU, VST3, and Standalone**.

---

## 🚀 Download & Install
1. Go to the [latest release](https://github.com/RemiBlaze/ClampitRaw/releases/latest).
2. Download **`ClampitRaw_Installer.pkg`**.
3. Double-click it and follow the installer. Signed & notarized by Apple — installs cleanly, no security warnings.
4. Restart your DAW and rescan plug-ins.

Full guide: **[remiblaze.com/support](https://remiblaze.com/support/)**.

---

## 🎛️ What It Does
One big **CLAMP** knob. Turn it up and Clampit Raw drives your signal harder into a lower brick-wall ceiling — pinning the peaks for a tighter, denser sound — while constant-loudness compensation keeps the output level-matched, so it just gets more controlled instead of louder. Under the hood it runs true-peak limiting with lookahead and a clean hard-knee clamp; a DC blocker and 0 dBFS safety ceiling keep the output honest. Great on the master bus, drums, and full mixes.

That single knob is all there is — no menus to dig through.
